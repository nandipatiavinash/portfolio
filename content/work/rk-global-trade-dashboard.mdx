## The problem

Fragmented operations on the factory floor led to inventory bottlenecks and missed deadlines. Staff had to manually double-record fabric logs from the looms onto spreadsheets, causing severe data entry lag and scheduling mistakes.

## The solution

A custom ERP tailored specifically to textile manufacturing workflows. The system enables real-time production logging directly at each loom, handles automatic raw material inventory depletion, and unifies customer invoicing in a single database.

## Key Features

1. **Raw Material Tracking:** Real-time monitoring of polymer and yarn inventory stages, from raw material intake at the warehouse to loom assignment on the production floor.
2. **Loom ID Logging:** Designed an intuitive interface for floor workers to log daily production meters and fabric roll codes directly against specific Loom IDs.
3. **Order Management:** A streamlined planning pipeline connecting live raw materials availability directly with active customer orders and delivery timelines.

## Results

Integrating loom-level entry with billing has eliminated order loss and given management 100% production visibility. Automated raw material logs increased overall inventory efficiency by 45%, helping RK Polymers schedule loom runs with far higher accuracy.
