## The problem

Handling complex stylist commission splits, tracking package memberships, and resolving cash register errors across disconnected tools was a massive daily admin overhead. The salon lacked a central dashboard to track daily performance and manage customer retention.

## The solution

We built a custom salon CRM and POS management platform that integrates billing, customer profiles, shift rosters, and stylist commissions into one fast, web-based dashboard.

## Key Features

1. **POS Billing:** High-speed check-out screens optimized for mobile or tablet use. Features native support for gift vouchers, membership packages, and multi-tier discount codes.
2. **Stylist Commission Calculator:** An automated commission engine that calculates exact payouts for stylists in real time based on services completed, tier structures, and tips.
3. **WhatsApp Invoicing:** Direct integration sending digital invoices, service summaries, and upcoming booking reminders directly to customers' WhatsApp numbers post-checkout.

## Results

Cashier entry errors and administrative calculation times dropped by 30%. The CRM’s membership ledger and simplified automated WhatsApp reminders drove a 22% increase in repeat customer bookings over the first quarter following launch.
